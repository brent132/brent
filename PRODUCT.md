# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The portfolio belongs to a full-stack web developer. Its primary users are visitors evaluating the developer's work and experience and deciding whether to make contact.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio helps visitors review selected work, understand the developer's experience, and contact the developer. Success means a visitor can quickly assess the developer's capabilities and has a clear path to start a conversation.

## Positioning

The developer uses AI agents as part of the full-stack development workflow to deliver work faster while keeping it efficient and clean.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the portfolio to inspect project work, review professional experience, learn about the developer, and find contact or social channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio must support work/project presentation, experience, an about or biography area, contact details, a resume, and social links.
- The product is expected to expand over time; additional content and capabilities remain open decisions.
- Exact project content, biography, resume, contact method, social profiles, and the developer's full public name have not been supplied yet.

## Brand Commitments

The developer's name or personal brand, biography, project content, resume, and social links are durable source material once supplied and must be preserved accurately. No claims or credentials should be invented.

## Evidence on Hand

No portfolio projects, work history, biography, resume, testimonials, client evidence, contact details, or social profiles are present in the repository yet. The current interface is the default Next.js scaffold and is not product evidence.

## Product Principles

- Make the developer's work and experience easy to assess.
- Keep a direct, obvious path to contact.
- Demonstrate fast, efficient, and clean execution.
- Explain the AI-agent-assisted workflow as a meaningful professional advantage.
- Keep the product extensible as real content and capabilities are added.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility requirements have been established yet; they remain an open decision.
